# License change – prospective scope

Cube Crashers is changing the license for new project versions from MIT to the PolyForm
Noncommercial License 1.0.0, effective with the first release that explicitly announces this
change and includes the new license notice.

This change does not revoke, amend or narrow rights already granted for copies previously
distributed under MIT. Recipients may continue to rely on the MIT License for those copies,
subject to its terms.

The public Git history contains earlier MIT-licensed code. This change does not make historical
copies or commits unavailable under MIT. A fork based on an earlier MIT release may retain rights
granted with that release. Read this note together with the license and release notes for the
specific version.

commercially **only as part of Cube Crashers and its official versions, updates, expansions,
ports, bundles and promotion**. This permits paid game releases, including distribution through
commercial game stores. It does not permit standalone resale or licensing of the contribution
as an unrelated asset, library or source-code package.

Contributions are also made available to the community as part of the project under the
