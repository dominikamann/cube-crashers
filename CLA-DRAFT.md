# Contributor License Agreement (CLA) – DRAFT ONLY

> **Not for execution.** This is a discussion outline, not legal advice or a finalized agreement.
> Have a qualified lawyer review and complete it for the correct rights holder, jurisdiction and
> acceptance process before merging contributions under a dual-licensing model.

A final CLA should expressly cover:

1. **Ownership:** the contributor retains ownership of their contribution.
2. **Copyright grant:** a worldwide, non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to reproduce,
   modify, create derivative works, publicly display, perform, distribute and make available the
   contribution.
3. **Commercial relicensing and sublicensing:** explicit authority for the project rights holder
   to offer the contribution, including as part of Cube Crashers, under separate commercial terms
   and to sublicense it to customers, distributors and publishing partners.
4. **Patent license:** a suitable license for contributor-controlled patent claims necessarily
   infringed by the contribution or its use.
5. **Representations:** authority to grant the rights, originality or proper authorization, and
   disclosure of known third-party restrictions.
6. **Employer and third-party material:** treatment of work created during employment or for
   another party, and of third-party code, data, assets or confidential information.
7. **No obligation to use:** the project rights holder need not use or maintain a contribution.
8. **Legal terms:** locally appropriate warranties, liability, governing law, venue, severability
   and electronic acceptance.

**This outline does not itself grant any rights.** A DCO alone is not a substitute where commercial
relicensing rights are required. Keep an auditable record of each contributor's explicit acceptance.
